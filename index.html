Ocean spatial knowledge for decision-makers

The Hydrospatial Literacy Platform gives marine practitioners, planners, and policymakers the knowledge to understand, interpret, and act on ocean spatial data. Five knowledge clusters. A searchable glossary of 80+ terms. A directory of 35 hydrospatial datasets for the region. Free, open access.
