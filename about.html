About this platform

Open-access literacy resources for practitioners and policymakers working with hydrospatial data in the Western Indian Ocean.

What it is

A knowledge platform for ocean data

The Hydrospatial Literacy Platform is an open-access resource built to help marine sector practitioners, policymakers, and students in Seychelles and the wider Western Indian Ocean understand, access, and apply hydrospatial data.

Hydrospatial science covers the measurement, mapping, and analysis of the ocean environment. The decisions that depend on it, from maritime boundary delimitation to blue economy planning and climate adaptation, require a working literacy in what the data is, where it comes from, what its limitations are, and how it reaches decision-makers. This platform is designed to build that literacy.

Knowledge clusters

Five areas of focus

Content is organised into five interconnected clusters, each addressing a distinct layer of the hydrospatial knowledge chain.

Cluster 1 Hydrospatial Foundations
Cluster 2 The Seabed and Water Column
Cluster 3 Data Types and Quality
Cluster 4 Marine Spatial Data Infrastructure
Cluster 5 From Data to Decisions

Phase 1 scope

What is in this platform

Phase 1 delivers five knowledge clusters with articles and supporting content, a sitewide glossary of hydrospatial terms, a verified directory of hydrospatial datasets relevant to the Western Indian Ocean, and reference resources including a physical and digital card deck (in development).

All content is open access. Registration is optional and allows you to receive updates as new content is published. No login is required to read any article, search the glossary, or browse the data directory.
