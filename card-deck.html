Hydrospatial Card Deck

A physical and digital reference deck for practitioners working with marine spatial data in Seychelles and the Western Indian Ocean.

Coming in Phase 2

Under development

The card deck is in design. It will be available as both a printed deck and a digital set, structured around the five knowledge clusters of this platform.

Each card covers one concept: a term, a dataset, a decision method, or a worked Western Indian Ocean example. The deck is designed for use in fieldwork, planning meetings, and training sessions without internet access.

Planned card sets

  • ● Cluster 1: Hydrospatial Foundations (coordinate systems, datums, reference surfaces)
  • ● Cluster 2: The Seabed and Water Column (survey methods, bathymetric products)
  • ● Cluster 3: Data Types and Quality (sensors, flags, fitness for purpose)
  • ● Cluster 4: Marine Spatial Data Infrastructure (metadata, interoperability, stewardship)
  • ● Cluster 5: From Data to Decisions (evidence chains, monitoring, MSP)
  • ● Glossary reference cards (key terms with Seychelles use notes)

Register on the platform to be notified when the card deck is available. Registered users receive access to the digital version on release.
