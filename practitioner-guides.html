Practitioner Guides

Six role-based guides for practitioners working with hydrospatial data in Seychelles and the Western Indian Ocean. Each guide maps the platform's knowledge clusters and workbook exercises to the data questions most relevant to that role.

These guides are in development. Role descriptions, cluster mappings and exercise recommendations will be refined through stakeholder review before the platform's public launch.
Marine Spatial Planner
Role context

Responsible for developing and implementing marine spatial plans under the Seychelles Maritime Zone Act and related instruments. Work involves integrating multiple data sources across habitat, bathymetry, and human use layers to produce legally defensible spatial designations.

Core hydrospatial questions

Which spatial datasets are authoritative for zone boundary delineation? How do I document the evidence chain from raw data to a plan boundary? What data gaps affect defensibility of a proposed designation?

Hydrographic Surveyor
Role context

Collects and processes bathymetric, tidal, and seabed data for navigation, infrastructure, and environmental applications. Works within or alongside the Seychelles Maritime Safety Administration and is responsible for data quality assurance and IHO standard compliance.

Core hydrospatial questions

How do I document datum and tidal reduction choices so downstream users understand the reference surface? What quality flags should accompany survey products shared with other agencies? How do I explain IHO order categories to non-technical decision makers?

GIS Specialist
Role context

Manages spatial datasets for a government agency or NGO, produces maps and analysis products for planning and reporting, and maintains the institutional GIS library. Often the first point of contact when other practitioners need help locating or combining data layers.

Core hydrospatial questions

How do I identify and document CRS mismatches before an overlay analysis? What licence conditions apply to data I am combining from multiple sources? How do I build a reusable data register that other colleagues can maintain?

Fisheries Manager
Role context

Advises on and implements fisheries management measures under the Fishing Act and related IOTC obligations. Needs to relate stock assessment outputs, vessel monitoring system data, and habitat maps to management decisions and reporting requirements.

Core hydrospatial questions

What spatial evidence supports a proposed seasonal closure or gear restriction? How do I communicate data uncertainty to the Fisheries Tribunal or the IOTC? Which habitat datasets are reliable enough to delineate essential fish habitat in a management plan?

Conservation Officer
Role context

Monitors marine protected areas, manages habitat data, and coordinates with enforcement and community liaison programmes. Works under the Environment Protection Act and the Protected Areas Act, and contributes to CBD and BBNJ reporting.

Core hydrospatial questions

How do I confirm that an MPA boundary in a global database matches the gazette boundary? What data do I need to establish a defensible baseline for effectiveness monitoring? How should I handle habitat maps produced by different projects with different methods?

Maritime Policy Officer
Role context

Advises on treaty obligations, negotiates bilateral and multilateral maritime instruments, and briefs senior officials on the spatial and legal implications of boundary claims and resource agreements. Needs to translate technical data limitations into policy risk.

Core hydrospatial questions

What constitutes sufficient hydrographic evidence for an UNCLOS Article 76 continental shelf submission? How do I communicate the difference between an operational chart and a legal boundary to a non-technical minister? What data management obligations attach to joint development agreements?
